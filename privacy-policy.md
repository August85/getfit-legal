# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Get Fit ("the app", "we") helps you track your health and fitness on your iPhone. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it is stored, and who it is shared with. The short version: **your health data stays on your iPhone and in Apple Health. We don't have a server that stores it, we don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.**

## Data the app uses

- **Apple Health data.** With your permission, the app reads sleep, weight, body fat, height, steps, distance, flights climbed, workouts, exercise minutes, active and resting energy, blood pressure, heart rate, heart rate variability, nutrition (calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat) and water, plus your date of birth and sex. It writes the weight, blood pressure, pulse, water, meals, manual workouts and height you log. You choose each type in Apple's permission screen and can change it at any time in the Health app.
- **Things you log in the app.** Meals and food items, journal notes, goals, reminder settings, manual readings, and profile details (name, date of birth, sex, height, weight, activity level).
- **Photos you choose to log.** Photos of meals, blood pressure monitors, scales or other displays, if you use photo logging.
- **Account information.** If you sign in with Apple: an anonymous Apple user ID and, if you share it, your email address or Apple's private relay address. No health data is linked to your account.
- **AI provider settings.** The provider, model and API key you enter for photo logging.

## Where your data is stored

- **Apple Health data** stays in Apple Health on your device. If you use iCloud Health sync, Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. The app never stores Health data in iCloud itself.
- **Data you log in the app** is stored in a database on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection (encrypted when your phone is locked).
- **Photos** are kept on your iPhone only if you choose "Keep photo" in Profile › Privacy. Otherwise they are discarded after logging.
- **Your AI API key** is stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only. It is not included in iCloud Keychain sync.
- We do not operate any server that receives or stores your health data, meals, notes, goals or photos.

## When data leaves your iPhone

- **Photo logging (optional).** When you tap "Read photo", the app sends that photo (resized on your phone), the category you picked and any hint you typed **directly to the AI provider you chose** (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or another compatible service), using your own API key. Nothing else is sent: not your name, account, Apple Health data or other readings. Before the first upload, and whenever you switch providers, the app asks for your consent and names the recipient. The provider's handling of these requests is governed by its own terms and privacy policy for your API account; the app links to them in Settings.
- **Sign in with Apple.** Apple handles sign-in under its privacy policy.
- **Exports you create.** If you export your data, the files go wherever you choose to share or save them.

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share personal data. If crash reporting is added in future, it will be configured to exclude health values, and this policy will be updated first.

## Health data and Apple's rules

Data from Apple Health is used only to provide the app's features to you. It is never used for advertising, data mining or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties except the AI provider you choose for a photo you ask the app to read.

## Your choices and rights

- **Access and export:** Profile › Data › Export creates CSV and JSON files of everything the app holds.
- **Delete:** Profile › Data › Delete all app data removes everything the app stores on your iPhone, with an option to also delete the readings the app wrote to Apple Health. Delete account also removes your account record.
- **Permissions:** change Apple Health access in the Health app, camera and photo access in iOS Settings, and revoke AI consent or remove your API key in Profile.
- Because we don't hold your data on a server, we can't recover it if your phone is lost. Back up with an encrypted iPhone backup or regular exports.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data. Since your data is stored on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app. For anything else, contact us using the details below.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). AI providers generally require API account holders to be at least 18, so photo logging asks you to confirm your age.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and show the new version in the app before the change applies to you.
