# Get Fit Support

Need help with Get Fit, found a problem, or have a question about your data?

Email **[abhimanyu.katyayan@gmail.com](mailto:abhimanyu.katyayan@gmail.com)**. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Profile › About).

## Common questions

- **My Garmin or Whoop data doesn't appear.** Turn on Apple Health sync in the Garmin Connect or Whoop app, and check that Get Fit can read that data type in Health › your profile › Apps › Get Fit.
- **Photo logging says my API key isn't valid.** Open Profile › AI provider, remove the key, copy it again from your provider's key page and tap Paste. Compare the characters shown with your provider's key list.
- **How do I export or delete my data?** Profile › Data. Export creates CSV and JSON files; Delete removes everything Get Fit stores on your iPhone.
